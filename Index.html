Important Dates in Nigerian History

1914: Amalgamation of Northern and Southern Nigeria under British administration.

1 October 1960: Nigeria gains independence from Britain.

1 October 1963: Nigeria becomes a republic.

1967–1970: Nigerian Civil War.

29 May 1999: Nigeria begins its current Fourth Republic.
